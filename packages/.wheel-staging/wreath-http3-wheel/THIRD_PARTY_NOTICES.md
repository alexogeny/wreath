The `wreath-http3` wheel dynamically links and bundles these independently
maintained libraries. They are build inputs for the opt-in HTTP/3 companion;
they are not dependencies of the `wreath` distribution.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| nghttp3 | 1.8.0 | MIT | <https://github.com/ngtcp2/nghttp3/tree/v1.8.0> |
| ngtcp2 | 1.25.0 | MIT | <https://github.com/ngtcp2/ngtcp2/tree/v1.25.0> |

The complete upstream license text for each bundled build is included in the
wheel's `licenses/` directory alongside Wreath's MPL-2.0 license.
