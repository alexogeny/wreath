How to read this report

  • Green is the best value in a row, red the worst — but only when the gap is real (at least 5%). An uncoloured field is one where the difference is too small to mean anything.
  • Ranges (hover a value, or the small figure beside it) show the spread across repeated runs. A number is only trustworthy next to its spread: a scenario's run-to-run variation is routinely larger than the gap being argued about.
  • A row reads unresolved or within noise when the leader's advantage is smaller than that spread. No winner is crowned there — overlapping ranges are a tie, whatever the medians happen to say.
  • A real win means the leader's worst sample still beats the runner-up's best one. A single run has no spread, so nothing in a one-run report can be told apart from noise.
  • Nothing is ranked across protocols (a request is different work in HTTP/1.1 and HTTP/3) or across load generators, and an errored row is never crowned. Flask is adapted from WSGI to ASGI, so its number carries the adapter's cost.
  • This is a shared-machine development tool for sensing direction — not a published, independently-generated comparison.
